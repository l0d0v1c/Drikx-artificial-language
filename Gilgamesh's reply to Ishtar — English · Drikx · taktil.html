Gilgamesh's Reply to Ishtar

Epic of Gilgamesh, Tablet VI — the king of Uruk refuses the goddess's hand. Twelve incisions.
᛫ ᛭ ᛫

No, I will not take you for a wife.

𐌗ᛆ ᛫ 𐌊ᚢ𐌓𐌕ᛆ𐌌ᚢ ᛫ 𐌍ᛆ ᛫ 𐌕ᛆ ᛫ 𐌕ᛁ ᛫ 𐌗ᚢ ᛫ 𐌌ᛁ𐌋ᛆ𐌓 ᛭

xa kurt-a-mu na ta ti xu milar

The whole refusal lives in xa; one's own will is stated in the direct evidential (-mu).

You are but a short-lived blaze,

𐌔ᛆ𐌕ᛆ𐌊ᛆ ᛫ 𐌕ᛁ ᛫ 𐌊ᛁ𐌓ᚢ𐌕 ᛫ ᛁ ᛫ 𐌐𐌋ᛁ𐌔𐌕 ᛭

sat-a-ka ti kirut i plist

"A flame of an instant" — kirut names both the flame and the ash.

A rickety door that keeps out
Neither wind nor rain.

𐌔ᛆ𐌕ᛆ𐌊ᛆ ᛫ 𐌕ᛁ ᛫ ᛁ𐌍𐌊ᛁ𐌋 ᛫ 𐌙ᛆ𐌊ᛆ𐌓 ᛫ 𐌊ᛁ ᛫ 𐌗ᛆ ᛫ 𐌊𐌋ᛆ𐌕ᛆ𐌊ᛆ𐌊ᛆ ᛫ 𐌕ᛆ ᛫ 𐌔ᚢ𐌓ᛆ𐌓 ᛫ 𐌔ᛆ ᛫ 𐌋ᚢ𐌔𐌕ᚢ𐌕 ᛫ ᚢ ᛫ 𐌔ᛆ ᛫ 𐌐ᛆ𐌋𐌔 ᛭

sat-a-ka ti inkil kʼakar ki xa klatak-a-ka ta surar sa lustut u sa pals

You are a palace whose roof
Threatens to fall upon its guests;

𐌔ᛆ𐌕ᛆ𐌊ᛆ ᛫ 𐌕ᛁ ᛫ 𐌐ᚢ𐌓𐌊 ᛫ 𐌐𐌓ᚢ𐌋ᛁ𐌌 ᛫ 𐌊ᛁ ᛫ 𐌍ᚢ ᛫ 𐌇ᚢ𐌋𐌊ᛆ𐌊ᛆ ᛫ 𐌔ᛁ𐌓𐌌ᛆ𐌋 ᛫ ᛁ ᛫ 𐌊ᚢ ᛫ 𐌗ᚢ ᛫ 𐌔𐌐ᛁ𐌋ᛆ𐌌ᛆ𐌓 ᛫ ᛁ ᛫ 𐌊ᚢ ᛭

sat-a-ka ti purk prulim ki nu hulk-a-ka sirmal i ku xu spilamar i ku

A threat is an inferred future: nu forces -ka.

You are the headband that smothers
The one who wears it;

𐌔ᛆ𐌕ᛆ𐌊ᛆ ᛫ 𐌕ᛁ ᛫ 𐌇ᛆ𐌔𐌕 ᛫ ᛁ ᛫ 𐌊ᛆ𐌓𐌕 ᛭
ᛆ𐌓ᛁ𐌋ᚢ𐌔ᛆ𐌊ᛆ ᛫ 𐌍ᛆ𐌓𐌊 ᛫ 𐌊ᛁ ᛫ 𐌔𐌕ᛁ𐌓𐌌ᛆ𐌋𐌐ᛆ𐌊ᛆ ᛫ 𐌕ᛆ ᛫ 𐌊ᚢ ᛭

sat-a-ka ti hast i kart ; arilus-a-ka nark ki stirmalp-a-ka ta ku

Drikx has no causative: "you are the head-cloth; short of air is the man who dons it".

You are the pitch that defiles
Whoever touches it;

𐌔ᛆ𐌕ᛆ𐌊ᛆ ᛫ 𐌕ᛁ ᛫ 𐌔ᚢ𐌌𐌐 ᛫ 𐌌ᚢ𐌓𐌊 ᛫ 𐌊ᛁ ᛫ 𐌕𐌓ᚢ𐌋𐌊ᛆ𐌊ᛆ ᛫ 𐌕ᛆ ᛫ 𐌍ᛆ𐌓𐌊 ᛫ 𐌊ᛁ ᛫ 𐌐𐌋ᛁ𐌊ᛆ𐌊ᛆ ᛫ 𐌕ᛆ ᛫ 𐌊ᚢ ᛭

sat-a-ka ti sump murk ki trulk-a-ka ta nark ki plik-a-ka ta ku

You are the waterskin that spills
Over the one who carries it;

𐌔ᛆ𐌕ᛆ𐌊ᛆ ᛫ 𐌕ᛁ ᛫ 𐌔𐌕ᛆ𐌊ᛁ𐌋 ᛫ 𐌊ᛁ ᛫ 𐌗ᚢ𐌋𐌍ᛆ𐌊ᛆ ᛫ 𐌔𐌕ᛆ𐌊 ᛫ ᛁ ᛫ 𐌊ᚢ ᛫ 𐌗ᚢ ᛫ 𐌊𐌓ᚢ𐌊ᛆ𐌓 ᛫ ᛁ ᛫ 𐌊ᚢ ᛭

sat-a-ka ti stakil ki xuln-a-ka stak i ku xu krukar i ku

No middle voice, no passive: it is the water that flows (xuln, a spontaneous process).

You are the limestone
That fouls the rampart;

𐌔ᛆ𐌕ᛆ𐌊ᛆ ᛫ 𐌕ᛁ ᛫ 𐌊ᛆ𐌋 ᛫ 𐌊ᛁ ᛫ 𐌕𐌓ᚢ𐌋𐌊ᛆ𐌔ᛁ𐌊ᛆ ᛫ 𐌕ᛆ ᛫ 𐌗ᛁ𐌋𐌐ᛆ𐌍 ᛭

sat-a-ka ti kal ki trulk-asi-ka ta xilpan

Repetitive aspect: the fouling happens deposit after deposit. The -asi epenthesis is written as it is spoken.

You are the battering ram
That pounds the city walls;

𐌔ᛆ𐌕ᛆ𐌊ᛆ ᛫ 𐌕ᛁ ᛫ 𐌊𐌓ᛆ𐌊ᛁ𐌋 ᛫ 𐌊ᛁ ᛫ 𐌊𐌓ᛆ𐌊𐌔ᛁ𐌊ᛆ ᛫ 𐌕ᛆ ᛫ 𐌗ᛁ𐌋𐌐ᛆ𐌍 ᛫ 𐌓ᚢ ᛭

sat-a-ka ti krakil ki krak-si-ka ta xilpan ru

The pounding lives entirely in the -si: striking in repetition.

You are the shoe too narrow
That bruises the foot.

𐌔ᛆ𐌕ᛆ𐌊ᛆ ᛫ 𐌕ᛁ ᛫ 𐌐𐌓ᛆ𐌋𐌕ᚢ𐌗 ᛫ 𐌊𐌓ᛆ𐌐 ᛫ 𐌊ᛁ ᛫ 𐌕𐌓ᛆ𐌌ᛆ𐌊ᛆ ᛫ 𐌕ᛆ ᛫ 𐌓ᛁ𐌍 ᛭

sat-a-ka ti praltux krap ki tram-a-ka ta rin

Is there a single one of your lovers
Whom you ever truly loved?

𐌇ᛆ ᛫ 𐌔ᛆ𐌕ᛆ ᛫ 𐌍ᛆ𐌕 ᛫ 𐌕ᛁ𐌓𐌐ᛆ𐌓 ᛫ ᛁ ᛫ 𐌕ᛁ ᛫ 𐌊ᛁ ᛫ 𐌐ᛆ ᛫ 𐌕ᛁ𐌓𐌐ᛆ ᛫ 𐌕ᛁ ᛫ 𐌔ᛆ ᛫ 𐌇ᛆ𐌓𐌕 ᛫ 𐌓ᛁ𐌊𐌕

ha sat-a nat tirpar i ti ki pa tirp-a ti sa hart rikt

A question takes no ᛭ closure — the final blank is where the answer belongs. "Truly": sa hart rikt, "from a true heart".

Is there one man anywhere
Whom you ever satisfied?

𐌇ᛆ ᛫ 𐌔ᛆ𐌕ᛆ ᛫ 𐌍ᛆ𐌕 ᛫ 𐌍ᛆ𐌓𐌊 ᛫ 𐌊ᛁ ᛫ 𐌐ᛆ ᛫ 𐌐ᛁ𐌍𐌔ᛆ ᛫ 𐌕ᛁ ᛫ 𐌕ᛆ ᛫ 𐌋ᚢ𐌓𐌐ᛁ𐌍 ᛫ 𐌔ᛆ ᛫ 𐌊ᚢ

ha sat-a nat nark ki pa pins-a ti ta lurpin sa ku

Agentive recast: "a man from whom you received pleasure" — the source is marked (sa ku).

The text. Gilgamesh's reply to the goddess Ishtar, who offered him marriage (Epic of Gilgamesh, Tablet VI). The king refuses, listing metaphor after metaphor the goddess's inconstancy toward her lovers — a text pressed into clay more than three thousand years ago, re-incised here in the stone of taktil.

Evidentiality. The whole invective is Gilgamesh's judgment: every assertion carries the inferential -ka, except the first line, where the speaker states his own will (-mu, direct). The two closing questions lose their evidential slot and remain unclosed.

Script. Taktil: consonants in Old Italic (the framework), vowels in runes (the binding), ᛫ word separator, ᛭ assertion closure. Every lexical word ends heavy, on a monumental glyph; every grammatical word ends light, on a rune. The page's single ejective, the trident-privative of 𐌙ᛆ𐌊ᛆ𐌓 (kʼakar "rickety"), is seen exactly as it is heard. Display: Old Italic needs a historic font (Segoe UI Historic, Noto Sans Old Italic).

Lexicon. New root: trulk "to defile, to foul". Cluster extensions: kal ⊃ limestone, sump murk ≈ pitch/bitumen, hast i kart ≈ headband, inkil ⊃ door, stakil ⊃ waterskin, krakil ⊃ battering ram.
